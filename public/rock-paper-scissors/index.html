How to play, in four steps

The rules of rock paper scissors stay the same: rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock. What changes is your hand.

1

10 cards each

You get 3 of two kinds and 4 of the third. Your opponent knows the split, but not which kind you have four of.

2

Pick & lock in

Each round you both choose one card and lock in within 10 seconds. Nobody sees a card until both are locked.

3

Count

Played cards are gone for good. As the hand shrinks you can work out what your opponent still holds.

4

Win the match

Ten rounds, one card each. Whoever wins more rounds wins the match. A level score is a draw.

Why 10 cards changes everything

Classic rock paper scissors is a coin flip with extra steps. Every throw is free, so there's nothing to count and nothing to plan. RPS 10 gives each player a hand they have to spend.

Because you hold 3 of two kinds and 4 of the third, every card you play tells your opponent something. By round six you are both counting what's left. By the last rounds you can sometimes know exactly what they have to play, and they know it too.

That's where the game lives: hiding which kind you hold four of, baiting your opponent into wasting their best card, and saving the right card for the final round. It's still rock paper scissors, but it rewards memory, reading people and nerve.

5 rock paper scissors strategy tips for RPS 10

Normal rock paper scissors strategy is mostly guessing. With 10 cards, real strategy kicks in.

  1. Count every card.The game shows what your opponent can still have, but keep your own count too. Once they've played three of a kind, they have at most one left of it.
  2. Hide your four.If you lead with your strongest kind, you tell them which one you have four of. Mix it in so they can't plan around it.
  3. Spend cards early to learn.The first rounds are cheap information. Watch what they open with: many players lead with their four.
  4. Save a finisher.Keep at least one card that beats what they're most likely to have left for the last rounds, when every choice is forced.
  5. Play the player.Many people stick with a card that just won and switch after a loss. Notice their habits, then lock in against them.

Frequently asked questions

Is RPS 10 free to play?

Yes. RPS 10 is free, with no ads and no download. Coins for the Store are earned by playing.

Can I play rock paper scissors online with friends?

Yes. Tap Friend in the game to open a room, then send the 5-letter code or the invite link. Your friend opens the link and you're in the same match. You can also add friends, see who's online and invite them straight from your friends list.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play straight away as a guest. Signing in with Google or with an email and password keeps your progress on every device and lets you pick your own username.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. RPS 10 runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone you can add it to your home screen to play it full-screen like an app.

How long is a match?

About two minutes. A match is 10 rounds, and you have 10 seconds to pick and lock in your card each round.

How is RPS 10 different from normal rock paper scissors?

Each player has only 10 cards: 3 of two kinds and 4 of the third. Every card you play is gone, so you can count what your opponent has left and plan ahead. It turns a game of luck into a game of memory, reading and bluffing.

Is there a ranked mode?

Yes. Ranked matches change your rating. Everyone starts at 1,000 and climbs from Bronze to Silver (1,100), Gold (1,200) and Diamond (1,350). Seasons last a calendar month.

What is the daily challenge?

Once a day everyone gets the same hand against the same bot, which plays its cards in the same order for everyone. You get one try. Play on back-to-back days to build a streak.

What happens if nobody else is online?

If no other player is found within about 15 seconds, you play a bot so you're never left waiting. Bots are clearly marked.

What happens if I run out of time?

In Ranked, a missed turn loses the round and a random card from your hand is discarded. In other modes, a random card is played for you.

Is the game fair?

Yes. The game server deals the cards and judges every round, so nobody can see your card before both players lock in. Playing the same opponent again and again counts for less in Ranked.

Can I chat with my opponent?

Only with quick reactions and set phrases like "gg" or "Nice one!". There is no free typing, and you can turn off your opponent's messages in Settings.

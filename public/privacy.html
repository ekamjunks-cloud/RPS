Privacy policy

RPS 10 is a free rock paper scissors game you play in your browser.

In short
  • We store your username, an account ID (if you sign in with Google or with an email and password), your game progress and any reports you send.
  • No ads, no selling data, no free-typed chat.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings › Delete account.

What we collect

Your username; if you sign in, an account ID from our sign-in service, Firebase (run by Google). Your email address and password are held by Firebase, not by the game: with Google sign-in, Firebase gets your email address and name from Google; with email sign-in, Firebase keeps your email address and a protected (hashed) copy of your password, which nobody can read back, including us. Your matches, rating, XP, coins, the items you own and your achievements; your friends list; player reports and bug reports you send (a bug report includes the game version and your browser and screen size). Guests' progress is linked to a random code stored in their browser.

Why

To run matches, keep your progress across devices, show leaderboards, and keep play fair (for example, reviewing reports of offensive names).

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the game: Google's Firebase (sign-in, and the password-reset and verify-your-email messages it sends) and SpacetimeDB (the game server). They process data only to run the game. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and there are no ads or ad trackers.

What other players see

Your username, avatar, aura, title, card style, rank and rating, your leaderboard place, and the quick-chat reactions and phrases you send during a match. There is no free typing between players.

Your choices

Play as a guest without signing in. Turn off your opponent's messages in Settings. Delete your account any time in Settings › Delete account: it is removed from the game server straight away, and your sign-in record is removed from Firebase too (if that step can't be done automatically, the game tells you, and we remove it when you email us). Reports you made about other players are kept, without your name, so moderation still works.

Storage on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser: your guest login, whether you're signed in, your settings (sound, music, vibration and so on) and whether you've seen the tutorial. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

RPS 10 has no free chat and collects only what's listed above. If you are a parent and want a child's account removed, use Delete account in the game or email us.

Contact

Email support@rps10.online, or use Report a bug in the game's Settings.

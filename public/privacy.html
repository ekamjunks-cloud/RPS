Privacy policy

RPS 10 is a free rock paper scissors game you play in your browser.

In short
  • We store your username, a sign-in ID from Google (if you sign in), your game progress and any reports you send.
  • No ads, no selling data, no free-typed chat.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings › Delete account.

What we collect

Your username; if you sign in with Google, an account ID from Google sign-in (your email address stays with Google sign-in and isn't stored in the game); your matches, rating, XP, coins, the items you own and your achievements; your friends list; player reports and bug reports you send (a bug report includes the game version and your browser and screen size). Guests' progress is linked to a random code stored in their browser.

Why

To run matches, keep your progress across devices, show leaderboards, and keep play fair (for example, reviewing reports of offensive names).

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the game: Google (sign-in, through Firebase) and SpacetimeDB (the game server). They process data only to run the game. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and there are no ads or ad trackers.

What other players see

Your username, avatar, aura, title, card style, rank and rating, your leaderboard place, and the quick-chat reactions and phrases you send during a match. There is no free typing between players.

Your choices

Play as a guest without signing in. Turn off your opponent's messages in Settings. Delete your account any time in Settings › Delete account: it is removed from the server straight away. Reports you made about other players are kept, without your name, so moderation still works.

Storage on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser: your guest login, your settings (sound, music, vibration and so on) and whether you've seen the tutorial. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

RPS 10 has no free chat and collects only what's listed above. If you are a parent and want a child's account removed, use Delete account in the game or email us.

Contact

Use Report a bug in the game's Settings to reach us. A support email address is coming soon.
